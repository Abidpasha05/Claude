Concept

Arkan (Arabic: أركان) means pillars / foundations / cornerstones. The mark is a stylized temple façade — four pillars supporting a lintel above and a stylobate below. The small accent dot at the centre is the warmth at the heart of every kitchen. The same geometric language carries through the wordmark "A".
