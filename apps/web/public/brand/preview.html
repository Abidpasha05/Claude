Concept

Aklio (rooted in أكل — Arabic for food / eating / a meal) is what happens when hospitality gets a modern operating system. The mark is a bold geometric monogram A: two confident triangular slabs in deep navy framing the customer journey, with a wide amber crossbar through the centre — the meal itself, the warmth at the heart of the brand. Navy gives Aklio authority and trust; amber carries the hospitality.
